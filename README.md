## philosophy 🤔
i am currently not using LLMs at all, in order to help me learn accurately and be a better critical thinker. i am NOT explicitly against LLM usage, but here's where i stand:
- i 100% favor local, on-device LLMs. i'm not too much of a privacy user, but i do like speed, reliability and to own MY data for MY uses - as well to have full control over the engine (eg: llama.cpp vs a capped UI for ChatGPT @ chat.com)
- due to their mathematical pattern-following (not really true thinking, rather a sophisticated next-token engine) nature, i refuse using it for complicated logic tasks (like Claude Code users do). as it is not reliable enough. i DO agree they are "good enough" for very basic completion "logic" (i.e.: turning an english prompt into simple function calls, like getting the current date or weather, or executing one or very few system commands/a shell script; or autocompleting single lines of code to assist in writing boilerplate)
- they are meant to be explicitly assistive and never replace ENTIRE jobs: not solely because of ethics, but because they are NOT that accurate with complicated logic, and the more you scale, the less chance you have shooting yourself in the foot BUT ALSO the worse a single fail can feel like. [you can put a robot to work flipping packages](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LewqAffahfQ) but not [a predictive token engine to manage your responsibilities](https://alexeyondata.substack.com/p/how-i-dropped-our-production-database)

my long term plan, related to that, is to investigate LLMs, by myself, in order to find 99-100% reliability (extremely high reliability when one-shot) use-cases that replace repetitive daily tasks. i truly believe current individuals and companies are looking at it the wrong way (forced scaling, not really understanding the math below, surrogating your own brains), not the correct and proven ways (explicitly assistive tasks: helping you understand a new library, autocompleting boilerplate, software that helps you find functionality in itself/fire it with NLP like happens in artistic software OR generating templates FOR YOU exclusively from YOUR WORK [i.e.: helping you quickly spin up a new "threejs project", "embedded programming audio player project in rp2040", "make a new DnB template"...])
